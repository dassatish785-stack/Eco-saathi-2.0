Eco Saathi 2.0 – Workflows
Version 1.0 · Draft · 15 October 2026 · Companion to the PRD and System Architecture
This document describes how customers, staff and systems move through Eco Saathi 2.0, step by step. Each workflow lists who acts, what happens and what the system does automatically.

2. Account and Login Workflow
Customer chooses "Login" or reaches it at checkout.
Enters phone number or email.
System sends a one-time password (OTP) and applies rate limits.
Customer enters the OTP; the system creates a session.
First-time customers are asked for their name and communication preferences (WhatsApp and email opt-ins, shown as separate unticked boxes).
Guest checkout is still allowed; guests can create an account after ordering using the same phone or email.

4. AI Assistant Conversation Flow
4.1 Main Flow
Greeting: the widget opens with a short welcome and quick buttons such as "Find a gift", "Plastic-free kitchen", "Track my order", "Talk to a person".
Understand the need: the assistant asks at most one clarifying question (for example, budget or use) before recommending.
Recommend: it searches the live catalogue and shows up to three product cards with price, stock and an "Add to cart" button.
Answer questions: for shipping, returns, materials and care it answers only from approved content, and links to the relevant policy page.
Order status: it asks for the order number and verifies the customer by an OTP sent to the phone or email on the order, then shows the status and tracking link.
Close: it asks whether the answer helped and offers a thumbs up or down with an optional comment.
4.2 Hand-Off Rules
The assistant hands over to a human when:
the customer asks for a person;
the question concerns a complaint, damaged item, refund dispute or payment problem;
it cannot find a reliable answer after two attempts;
the customer shows frustration, or the topic is outside the store's products and policies.
On hand-off, the system creates a support ticket with a conversation summary, notifies the support team on WhatsApp or email, and tells the customer the expected response time. Support hours are shown in the widget.
4.3 Things the Assistant Must Not Do
Guess prices, stock, delivery dates or policies.
Share another customer's information.
Make health, medical or environmental claims beyond those approved by the business.
Take payments or change an order without a verified customer and confirmation from the customer.

5.2 Customer-Started WhatsApp Chat
Customer taps the WhatsApp button, or "Chat about this product" on a product page.
WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message that includes the product name and link.
The support team (or, later, the assistant) replies within the 24-hour customer service window.
Messages and outcomes are logged against the customer's record where the phone number matches.
5.3 Consent Handling
Opt-in boxes are unticked by default and explain what will be sent.
Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link; WhatsApp supports a "STOP" reply.
Opt-outs take effect immediately and are stored with a timestamp.
Order-related messages (confirmation, shipping, refund) are service messages and are separate from marketing.
6. Returns and Refunds Workflow
Customer opens the order and selects "Request return or refund" within the policy period.
Chooses the item and reason, and uploads a photo if the item is damaged or wrong.
System checks eligibility against the published returns policy and creates a request.
Support reviews the request, usually within one working day, and approves, rejects (with reason) or asks for more information.
If approved, a pickup is booked with the courier, or the customer is told how to send the item back.
When the item is received and checked, the refund is issued to the original payment method, or by bank transfer for cash-on-delivery orders.
The customer receives a refund confirmation and the stock is updated if the item is resalable.
7. Corporate Gifting Workflow
Buyer opens the Corporate Gifting page and submits the enquiry form (company, contact, product interest, quantity, delivery date, customisation needs).
System stores the lead, sends an acknowledgement email, and notifies the sales team by email and WhatsApp.
Sales contacts the buyer within one working day, shares a quote and samples if needed.
Buyer approves the quote; an order is created in the admin panel with bulk pricing and a GST invoice.
Customisation artwork is approved, production and packing are done, and shipping follows the standard order lifecycle.
After delivery, the team asks for feedback and a repeat-order opportunity is noted in the lead record.
8. Staff and Admin Workflows
8.1 Adding or Updating a Product
Staff open the admin panel and create or edit a product.
Fill title, description, material, use tags, price, stock, weight, photos (with alt text) and the eco-impact note.
Save as draft, preview on a staging link, then publish.
System updates the search index and refreshes the product page and category pages.
8.2 Daily Order Handling
Review new orders and any payment or COD flags.
Pack items and generate the courier label from the admin panel.
Mark orders as shipped; customers are notified automatically.
Check the support inbox, hand-off tickets and returns queue.
Review low-stock alerts and update inventory.
8.3 Weekly Review
Check sales, top and slow products, conversion and abandoned-cart numbers.
Review chatbot questions that were unanswered or rated poorly, and add the missing content or fix the answer.
Moderate pending reviews and plan the newsletter.
9. Content and SEO Workflow
Draft a blog post or page in the admin editor with a target keyword.
Add title, meta description, image alt text and internal links to related products.
Preview, publish, and request indexing in Google Search Console.
Review search performance monthly and refresh underperforming pages.
