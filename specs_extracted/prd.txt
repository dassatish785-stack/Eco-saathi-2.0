Eco Saathi 2.0 – Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version 1.0 · Draft · 15 October 2026 · Website: ecosaathi.com
1. Overview
Eco Saathi is a Bhubaneswar-based store selling eco-friendly products made from bamboo, sabai grass, jute, neem wood and organic materials. The current store lists 37 products across five material-based categories and runs on a hosted store-builder platform (Typof). Eco Saathi 2.0 is the redevelopment of this website into a faster, easier-to-use, better-converting store with an AI shopping assistant, WhatsApp and email integration, and a redesigned product experience.
2. Problem Statement
An audit of the current Shop page found the following gaps:
SEO tags are missing or weak: empty meta and Open Graph descriptions, no Twitter card, and generic page titles.
Products are grouped only by material, so shoppers searching by need (oral care, kitchen, gifting) cannot find items easily. The only collection filter is "Toothbrush".
No visible product search, sorting, reviews or other trust signals.
Corporate gifting, a likely high-value channel, is hidden as the last menu item.
Support depends on a single floating WhatsApp button, with no automated assistance, order updates or email flows.
Content errors: a truncated menu label ("crisi"), a grammar issue in the cart ("1 items"), duplicate popup markup, and a footer with unreadable contact details.
The footer promotes cotton bags, but no cotton category exists.

7. Non-Functional Requirements
Performance: mobile Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 s; images in WebP or AVIF with lazy loading.
Availability: 99.5% or better monthly uptime.
SEO: unique title and meta description per page, structured data (Product, Breadcrumb, Organization), XML sitemap, clean URLs and 301 redirects from old URLs.
Accessibility: target WCAG 2.1 AA (alt text, contrast, keyboard navigation, readable link text).
Compatibility: latest two versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox; Android and iOS browsers.
Scalability: handle at least 10x current traffic during festival sales without redesign.
Security and privacy: as defined in the Security document.
