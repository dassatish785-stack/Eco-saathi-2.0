About Eco Saathi

Together, We Can Make a Difference.

In Hindi and Odia, Saathi means a loyal companion or trusted friend. Eco Saathi was founded on the belief that everyday sustainability shouldn’t feel like an overwhelming sacrifice or an overpriced luxury.

We partner directly with indigenous artisans, tribal women’s self-help groups (SHGs), and green agroforestry farmers across Odisha to replace disposable fossil plastics with purposeful, beautiful essentials carved from bamboo, neem wood, riverbank sabai grass, and circular crop residue.

Our Core Philosophy

Why Sustainability?

Sustainability should not be an expensive luxury or an inconvenient compromise. We are making eco-conscious everyday choices accessible, affordable, and practical for every Indian household.

Bamboo: Nature's Wonder Grass

Water- and energy-efficient bamboo absorbs 40% more CO₂ and produces 35% more oxygen than an equivalent area of tree forests. Bamboo matures in just 3–4 years without toxic fertilizers, making it the supreme renewable alternative to fossil plastics.

✦ Used in: Toothbrushes, Flasks, Straws, Earbuds, Facial Tissues, and Stationery.

Medicinal Neem: Antibacterial Protection

Neem trees thrive in semi-arid soils with zero artificial irrigation. Revered for millennia in Ayurveda, neem wood possesses natural antifungal, antibacterial, and anti-static properties that nourish scalps and prevent microplastic leaching into human bodies.

✦ Used in: Wide-Tooth Detangler Combs, Handle Combs, Shampoo Combs, and Soap Dishes.

Crop Residue & Agricultural Biomass

By upcycling agricultural straw and husks into durable biocomposites, we directly prevent crop stubble burning — a primary cause of seasonal smog and air pollution across northern and eastern India.

✦ Used in: Wheat Straw Toothbrushes, Organic Bath Loofahs, and Natural Scrubbers.

Mayurbhanj Sabai Grass & Golden Jute

Known as "Golden Grass", Sabai grows wild along riverbanks in Mayurbhanj, Odisha. Woven by indigenous tribal women's self-help groups (SHGs), Sabai grass and coastal Jute provide durable, 100% biodegradable replacements for plastic packaging and synthetic homeware.

✦ Used in: Storage Baskets, Planters, Coasters, Dining Mats, and Juco Totes.
